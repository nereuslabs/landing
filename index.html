The Socratic Method vs. Standard AI

Orchestration

The 4-Agent Lifecycle

A disciplined pipeline moving from vulnerability detection to verified mastery.

01 / DIAGNOSTIC

Adaptive Quiz

Generates an adaptive diagnostic quiz and evaluates answers to produce a precise WeaknessReport.

02 / COACH

Goal Roadmap

Orders your learning path by weak areas, building a dynamic, goal-oriented milestone tree tailored to your skill level.

03 / TUTOR

Katas & Theory

Delivers conceptual theory and hands-on coding katas designed to tackle specific architectural and language nuances.

04 / EXAMINER

Sandboxed Verdict

Runs sandboxed test executions and provides difficulty-scaled Socratic hints that prompt understanding without giving away code.
